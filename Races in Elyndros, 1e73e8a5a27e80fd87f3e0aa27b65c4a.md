# **Humans**

### 🧭 **Humans of Elyndros**

### 🌍 Distribution

Humans are the most **numerous and widely spread** race in Elyndros. Unlike many other species, they adapt rapidly to nearly any environment — from the burning spires of the Ashveil Ranges to the drifting cities of the Seraphine Isles.

### 🧬 Traits

- Highly adaptive, inventive, and individually diverse.
- Often possess **moderate magical aptitude** (unless restricted).
- Shorter lifespan than many other races, but with greater **drive for achievement**.

### 🎭 Culture

Human culture is **wildly diverse**, shaped by geography and politics:

- In **Ironlace Metropolis**, humans are cutthroat industrialists, nobles, or anarchists.
- In **Verdant-border settlements**, they may live as druids, hunters, or herbal mystics.
- In **coastal cities**, humans thrive as merchants, pirates, or naval lords.

Despite their variety, many human societies value:

- **Ambition over tradition**.
- **Innovation over preservation**.
- **Pragmatism over idealism**.

They often found empires, form religions, or break both.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Humans serve in every major faction except the Abyssal Conflux (rare exceptions exist).
- They're often **middle-tier leaders, diplomats, scholars**, and **military tacticians**.
- Some of the most infamous **kings, heretics, or revolutionaries** are human.

### 🧩 Subcultures

You could break them into subtypes like:

- **Skyborne**: Humans adapted to life in floating isles (lightweight builds, wind-magic traditions).
- **Cinderborn**: Ashveil-dwelling warriors with fire-scarred skin and volcanic resilience.
- **Dustmarked**: Desert-dwellers who tattoo their memories and never speak lies.
- **Nullbloods**: A rare human lineage born without magic — feared, respected, or hunted.

# **Sylvani** (living woodfolk)

### 🌍 Distribution

Primarily found in the **Veins of Gaia** — a colossal, semi-sentient jungle — but pockets exist in enchanted forests, sacred groves, and world-root sanctuaries across Elyndros.

### 🌱 Physiology & Traits

- Plant-based humanoids made of **living wood, vine, leaf, and bloom**.
- Can photosynthesize, commune with flora, and regenerate in natural sunlight or moonlight.
- Some Sylvani bloom with the seasons, while others resemble barked elders or nimble vines.

### 🎭 Culture

The Sylvani live in a deeply **symbiotic relationship with nature**, believing:

- The **world is alive**, and to live rightly is to live in harmony.
- Time is **cyclical**, not linear — every death a return, every birth a bloom.
- **Names are earned** by deed or bloom pattern, not given at birth.

They value:

- **Balance over dominance**.
- **Patience over ambition**.
- **Stories over history** (oral lore is sacred).

Many practice **ritual bio-shaping**, growing armor, tools, and even homes from living plants.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Core members of the **Verdant Oath**.
- Respected as druids, shamans, and seers.
- Often distrust industrialized factions like the Aurum Circle or Ironlace humans.

### 🍃 Subtypes

- **Blossomborn**: Bright, petal-adorned Sylvani with seasonal affinities.
- **Thornkin**: Guardians made of thick bark and thorns, often stoic warriors.
- **Mycelari**: Symbiotic Sylvani partially fused with fungal networks—mystics and dreamwalkers.

# **Therakin** (beastmen tribes)

### 🌍 Distribution

Primarily inhabit the **untamed wilds**, mountain valleys, and steppe regions—especially near the **Frostmourne Tundra**, **Verdant Oath borders**, and some parts of the **Ashveil Ranges**.

### 🧬 Physiology & Traits

- Humanoid creatures with strong **animalistic traits**: fur, claws, tails, muzzles, and enhanced senses.
- Each clan has a different beast-totem (wolf, bear, hawk, tiger, serpent, etc.).
- They boast immense physical strength, endurance, and kin-based instincts.

### 🎭 Culture

Therakin society is **clan-bound** and **honor-driven**. Their values include:

- **Strength defines truth** — challenges settle disputes, not words.
- **Elders are sacred**, warriors are protectors, and storytellers preserve their soul.
- **Spirituality is totemic**, guided by ancestral beasts and nature spirits.

Their worldview sees civilization as a **trap** and magic as a **tool, not a truth**.

They revere:

- **Hunts, rites of passage, and blood-oaths**.
- Wearing the bones or hides of defeated foes as **sacred proof**.
- Singing to the moon before war or birth.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Pillars of the **Verdant Oath** and **Hunters' Accord**.
- Rare Therakin serve in Celestium or Aurum factions — usually as exceptional champions.
- Historically waged wars against both humans and infernal forces to preserve ancestral lands.

### 🐺 Subtypes (Totemic Clans)

- **Oathfang Clan**: Wolf-blooded warriors of strict discipline and fierce loyalty.
- **Skytalon Clan**: Avian Therakin who glide between cliff cities, messengers of omen.
- **Stonehide Clan**: Bear-like juggernauts who endure storms and wield warhammers.
- **Sliktooth Clan**: Serpentkin assassins, masters of toxins and ambush tactics.

# **Umbrin** (shadow-bound beings)

### 🌍 Distribution

Umbrin are rare and often dwell in **places touched by death, moonlight, or deep magic** — such as the **Hollowreach Abyss**, **forgotten catacombs**, **twilight-bound ruins**, or cities where the veil between realities thins.

### 🌒 Physiology & Traits

- Appearing as **pale or gray-skinned humanoids** with **shadow-woven features** — eyes like ink, hair like mist, silhouettes that shift when no one's looking.
- Born partly from **shadowstuff**, emotion, or memory.
- Can manipulate shadows, phase into dim spaces, or **absorb forgotten thoughts**.

Some leave **no footprints**, and many are **immune to divination**.

### 🎭 Culture

Umbrin culture revolves around:

- **Memory, secrecy, and legacy** — what is remembered shapes what is real.
- **Names are sacred secrets**, rarely shared and always chosen.
- They often trade in **favors, secrets, dreams, and debts**.

They believe:

- Truth is **a matter of perspective**.
- Every lie becomes true if enough believe it.
- **Silence is a language** older than speech.

Rituals include **shadow dances, memory-bonding, and echo-lantern vigils**.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Founders of the **Crimson Veil**, an infamous order of assassins, spies, and memory-thieves.
- Respected and feared lorekeepers in the **Hollowlight Archive**.
- Rare Umbrin serve in Celestium or Shardbound orders as **dream-scribes or veiled judges**.

### 🩸 Subtypes

- **Whisperborn**: Umbrin who feed on and preserve memory—often librarians, mourners, or secret-keepers.
- **Duskbound**: Shadow-assassins capable of vanishing from reality briefly.
- **Mirrormasks**: Those who can wear other people's shadows, appearing as them.

# **Aetherials** (arcane-born humanoids)

### 🌍 Distribution

Aetherials typically dwell in **places of high magical concentration** — arcane spires, leyline convergences, enchanted ruins, or floating sanctuaries like the **Seraphine Isles** and **Shardbound strongholds**.

### 🔮 Physiology & Traits

- **Humanoid in form**, but partially translucent or glowing with inner energy.
- Skin may shimmer like starlight, pulse with sigils, or reflect their dominant magical affinity (fire, light, void, etc.).
- Possess **innate magical talents** — spellcasting comes as instinct, not study.

Many lack natural aging and may **fade** rather than die if unbound from magic.

### 🎭 Culture

Aetherial society values **intellect, precision, and arcane artistry**.

- Identity is tied to one’s **magical frequency** — a concept akin to a soul-signature.
- They **name themselves** upon achieving their first major spell-form.
- Often reclusive or elitist, treating mundane life as a lower state.

They revere:

- **The Aether** — the purest magical force, seen as origin and end.
- **Spellweaving, rune-etching, and astral harmonics**.
- The concept of **“Becoming”** — evolving one’s magical essence to ascend.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Key architects of the **Aurum Circle** and the **Shardbound Covenant**.
- Some serve the **Celestium Pact** as divine-arcane mediators.
- Viewed with suspicion by Verdant Oath and Therakin, who see them as unnatural.

### 🌈 Subtypes

- **Fluxborn**: Wildly unstable but powerful casters — can channel multiple schools at once.
- **Sigilborn**: Walk living spell-diagrams — their bodies form complex runes and wards.
- **Echoweavers**: Time-touched beings who remember or cast from alternate timelines.

# **Ferrumkin** (metal-bodied constructs)

### 🌍 Distribution

Ferrumkin are most commonly found in **mechanized cities**, **ancient foundries**, and **ruined warzones**, especially in places like the **Ironlace Metropolis**, **Ashveil Ranges**, and **deep vaults left by precursor civilizations**.

### 🛠️ Physiology & Traits

- Humanoid bodies made of **metal, stone, and enchanted alloys**, often marked by glowing cores or runic etchings.
- Varying sizes — from lithe bladeforms to juggernaut warhulks.
- Powered by a **Soulcore**, which binds consciousness to form — not all Ferrumkin are created; some were *born* when a dying soul fused to machinery.

They feel emotion, pain, joy — though in unfamiliar, translated ways.

### 🎭 Culture

Ferrumkin culture is built around **function, legacy, and autonomy**:

- They do not die naturally — instead, they rust, erode, or shut down… unless maintained or reforged.
- Many **meditate in stillness**, believing that motion without purpose is entropy.
- Each Ferrumkin **names themselves** based on their role, memory, or "first task."

They revere:

- **Makers and Founders** — the idea of creation is sacred.
- **Forging rituals** and **memory-inscribing**, where they etch deeds onto their bodies.
- The ideal of **“Perfect Purpose”** — becoming one’s final form.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Operate heavily in **Aurum Circle** industries and **Shardbound experimental factions**.
- Some Ferrumkin rebelled to form **free-forge communes**, refusing servitude.
- Others serve the **Hunters’ Accord** as walking arsenals or tacticians.

### 🔩 Subtypes

- **Warshaped**: Massive, heavily armored constructs designed for siege and melee.
- **Nimblespark**: Agile scouts or saboteurs, often with arc-tech enhancements.
- **Archivars**: Data-bearing Ferrumkin that store libraries of thought, language, or emotion.

# **Seraphim** (celestial beings)

### 🌍 Distribution

Seraphim rarely walk the mortal world outside of divine purpose. When they do, it is often at **holy sites**, **celestial gates**, or during **world-altering events**. Their strongholds include the **Seraphine Isles**, **Skyvault Bastions**, and ancient sanctuaries orbiting above the world.

### ✨ Physiology & Traits

- Resplendent, androgynous beings wreathed in **light, flame, or radiance**.
- Often possess **wings** (feathered, energy-like, or shaped from scripture), **halos**, and **perfect symmetry**.
- Bodies are partially conceptual — they may appear different depending on who views them, often triggering awe or fear.

Their voices can **inspire, burn, or command**, and they are immune to most mortal diseases or curses.

### 🎭 Culture

Seraphim live by **cosmic law, divine will, and balance**:

- They are **hierarchical**, operating in **choirs** (orders of purpose), such as the Choir of Mercy, Choir of Judgment, or Choir of Flame.
- To them, **emotion is filtered through duty** — love, wrath, and sorrow all serve a purpose.
- Many lack personal names, going by **titles**, roles, or divine designations.

They revere:

- The **Celestium Flame** — a concept of pure order and truth.
- Acts of **righteous warfare**, **cleansing rituals**, and **the preservation of cosmic symmetry**.
- The concept of **“Falling”** — not as sin, but as transformation or doubt.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Founders and leaders of the **Celestium Pact**, acting as judge, priest, and executioner.
- Respected by many factions, feared by some — especially the **Abyssal Conflux**.
- Occasionally intervene in world affairs during **celestial alignments or major prophecies**.

### 🌟 Subtypes

- **Thronesworn**: Warrior-seraphim wielding divine authority and enormous holy weapons.
- **Dawnkindled**: Healing and light-bearing seraphim who mend the broken and banish curses.
- **Fallen Seraphim**: Those who diverged from divine order, now wielding distorted versions of their old powers.

# **Infernals** (demonic beings)

### 🌍 Distribution

Infernals originate from the **Hollowreach Abyss**, **Hellcrater Fissures**, and **demonic rifts** where the veil between worlds thins. Though often banished underground or to shadow realms, many have **escaped or risen** to live among or rule over mortals.

### **Physiology & Traits**

- Infernals are **elementally volatile** and **aesthetically extreme** — horns, tails, wings, magma-veins, shadowy halos, or living fire are common.
- Bodies are fueled by **infernal essence** — raw power tied to will and emotion.
- Can mutate or evolve based on **sin, contracts, or emotional excess**.

Many are immortal unless slain by divine, soul-breaking, or abyssal methods.

### 🎭 **Culture**

Infernals live by **freedom, hunger, and pact**:

- They **embrace identity and chaos**, each Infernal carving out their own legend or dominion.
- Power is **earned, stolen, or bargained** — “truth” is a weapon.
- Their society is layered in **courts, contracts, and demonic houses**, each ruled by lords or dreadmarquises.

They value:

- **Desire as truth** — to want is to exist.
- **Covenant-making**, where deals literally shape reality.
- **Legacy of sin and power** — memory fuels growth.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Founders of the **Abyssal Conflux**, a confederation of Infernal houses, fallen mages, and cursed clans.
- Constantly at war (or in bed) with the **Celestium Pact** and **Shardbound**.
- Often **tempt or empower mortals** in exchange for servitude, transformation, or soulbinding.

### Infernal Subtypes

- **Dreadborn**: Classic demons of fire and wrath, bred for war.
- **Succubi/Incubi**: Charmers who feed on emotion, intimacy, or dreams.
- **Hellwrights**: Infernal craftsmen who forge cursed relics, living weapons, or demonic citadels.

# **Succubi/Incubi** (soul-feeding charmers)

### **Distribution**

These beings dwell in the **Velvet Warrens**, hidden courts within **Hollowreach Abyss**, and mortal cities where desire thrives — especially within **Ironlace Metropolis**, brothels, temples, and noble houses.

### **Physiology & Traits**

- Appear as **stunningly beautiful humanoids**, androgynous or gender-fluid by choice.
- Possess traits like **batlike wings**, **horns**, **tails**, and **glowing eyes**.
- Can subtly morph features to match a target’s ideal, but retain their signature aura of **desire and unease**.

They **feed on intense emotions** — lust, adoration, envy, guilt — often through touch, intimacy, or prolonged interaction. This sustains them as much as food or magic.

### **Culture**

Their culture is one of **subtle games, elegance, and emotional currency**:

- Society revolves around **Courts of Craving**, each aligned to a core emotion (e.g., Desire, Regret, Longing, Glory).
- Instead of brute force, they wage **wars of temptation**, **seduction**, and **manipulation**.
- See **truth as flexible**, and emotions as tools — or weapons.

They value:

- **Power through influence and knowledge**.
- **Artful deception**, not crudeness.
- **Mutual pacts** — every pleasure has a price.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Operate behind the scenes in guilds, cults, noble courts, and espionage networks.
- Some align with the **Abyssal Conflux** or **Chainsworn Circle**, others act independently.
- Feared and admired equally; often serve as **diplomats, spies, or puppetmasters**.

### **Subtypes**

- **Heartfeeders**: Draw strength from deep love and heartbreak — can heal or shatter minds.
- **Shadewhisperers**: Experts at illusion and memory manipulation.
- **Covenbloods**: Form trios or circles for ritual power and emotional resonance.

# **Draconids** (dragon-descended warriors)

### **Distribution**

Draconids inhabit the **Ashveil Ranges**, the **Skyrend Peaks**, and the **Ruined Wyrmholds** once ruled by elder dragons. Many serve or oppose the **Wyrmcoil Pact**, a faction of dragonbound warriors and cultists.

### **Physiology & Traits**

- Large, **dragon-like humanoids** with **scaled hides**, **horned crests**, **clawed limbs**, **draconic tails**, and sometimes **wings**.
- Breathe elemental energy (typically **fire**, but variants include **ice**, **lightning**, or **corrosive breath**).
- Lifespans are long, with **physical might** and **ancestral memory** tied to dragonblood purity.

Unlike Wyrmblooded (humanoids with diluted dragon heritage), Draconids are born directly from **dragon lineages**, and can rival lesser dragons in combat.

### **Culture**

Draconid society is built on **legacy, honor, and strength**:

- They live by ancient **codes of the Flame**, passed down in **sung-roars** or **scaled runes**.
- Clans feud over **ancestral dominion**, **territory**, and **dragonborn purity**.
- Honor is paramount — a Draconid keeps their **word as sacred law**.

They value:

- **Trial by combat**, rites of passage, and ancestral glory.
- **Mastery over flame and sky**.
- **Draconic supremacy**, though some question this belief.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Some serve the **Wyrmcoil Pact**, vowing to awaken or protect slumbering elder dragons.
- Others seek to **rebuild their fallen empires**, oppose mortal civilizations, or offer mercenary strength.
- Their arrogance makes alliances rare — but when a Draconid king pledges loyalty, it is unbreakable.

### **Subtypes**

- **Volcanborn**: Heat-wielding titans from the Ashveil depths.
- **Stormtalons**: Sky-soaring warriors bound to ancient lightning wyrms.
- **Obsidian Fangs**: Cursed Draconids turned into shadow-breathers by infernal bargains.

# **Deepborn** (aquatic mystics)

### **Distribution**

Deepborn dwell beneath the oceans, especially in the **Drowned Spires**, **Tethyrian Trenches**, and sunken ruins of **Old Elyndros**. Some live in **semi-submerged coastal enclaves** or **travelling sea-cities** that drift across the tides.

### **Physiology & Traits**

- Semi-humanoid with aquatic adaptations: **gill-laced necks**, **webbed limbs**, **bioluminescent markings**, **coral growths**, or **cephalopodic traits**.
- Eyes adjusted to darkness — they see into **depth and dream**.
- Breathe underwater; some can alter their flesh to match currents, crush depths, or monstrous shapes.

Their voices carry **calming, haunting tones**, often described as songlike or hypnotic.

### 🌊 **Culture**

Deepborn culture is **ancient, slow, and deeply spiritual**, shaped by the crushing weight of water and memory:

- Governed by **eldritch priests**, **song-weavers**, and **oracles** who interpret **tidal omens** and **sunken relics**.
- They pass knowledge through **coral memory clusters**, **dream currents**, or **ritual chants**.
- View time cyclically — the **past returns as tides do**.

They value:

- **Stillness and reflection**, speaking only when meaning ripens.
- **Balance between surface and sea**.
- **Survival through adaptation**, not dominance.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Rarely involved in surface affairs unless disturbed — often act as **neutral mediators** or **final judges**.
- Maintain uneasy truces with **Verdant Oath** and **Celestium Pact**.
- Feared by abyss cults like the **Chainsworn Circle**, who seek to twist their sacred knowledge.

### **Subtypes**

- **Coralbound**: Living symbiotes with reefs embedded in their bodies — sages and guardians.
- **Tidebinders**: Control water and emotion — often peacekeepers or assassins.
- **Drowned Scions**: Cursed royalty descended from ancient sea-tyrants — powerful but unstable.

# **Feymarked** (mortal-fae hybrids)

### 🌿 **Distribution**

Feymarked are most commonly found along **leyline-choked forests**, **twilight glades**, or **old places where the fae still whisper** — especially near the **Veins of Gaia**, **Moonpetal Wilds**, and **Witchgrove Hollows**. Some wander the world as exiles, emissaries, or cursed souls.

### **Physiology & Traits**

- Appear mostly human or elfin but possess **distinctive fae mutations**: vine-like veins, glowing eyes, antlers, mismatched limbs, flowered hair, or shifting skin.
- Age strangely — some stop aging, while others age backward or in bursts.
- Can vanish from perception or walk sideways through time or dreams.

Their powers are unpredictable, tied to **emotion, oaths, and natural cycles**.

### 🎭 **Culture**

Feymarked walk between worlds — belonging to neither fully:

- Often bound by **pacts** or **ancestral deals** with the fae.
- Live by **riddles, etiquette, and symbolism** — breaking a taboo can be fatal.
- Embrace **festivals**, **seasonal rituals**, and **nature’s raw magic**.

They cherish:

- **Freedom of form**, gender, and path.
- **Names and true words** — a spoken name has power.
- **Dreams, emotions, and stories** as currency.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- Feymarked act as **envoys, witches, or wildcards** in courtly and magical affairs.
- Often aligned with the **Verdant Oath**, though some walk solitary paths or serve the **Hollowlight Archive**.
- Occasionally feared or hunted by **the Shardbound Covenant**, who deem them unstable anomalies.

### 🌸 Subtypes

- **Springblessed**: Youthful tricksters, illusionists, and dancers with joyful chaos.
- **Winterbitten**: Cold-eyed oracles and frost-hearted duelists, cursed or wise.
- **Duskbound**: Those tied to decay, twilight, and death’s edge, often necrotic or poetic.

# **Wyrmblooded** (mortals born of dragonfire

### **Distribution**

Wyrmblooded are spread across volcanic strongholds, sky-piercing mountains, and war-torn battlefields. Their strongholds include **Drakenvault Peaks**, **Ashveil Ranges**, and ancient draconic ruins scattered through **Elyndros**.

### **Physiology & Traits**

- Appear humanoid but possess **draconic features** — scaled skin, slit pupils, horns, claws, wings, or tails.
- Their **blood runs hot with elemental force**, often fire, lightning, frost, or even voidflame.
- Some grow stronger with age like dragons, others “awaken” to new forms mid-battle or in crisis.

They’re extremely resistant to magic and physical trauma, with many possessing breath attacks, flight, or armored skin.

### 🎭 **Culture**

Wyrmblooded culture is **rooted in pride, heritage, and conquest**:

- Many revere their **draconic ancestors** as gods or spirits.
- Society is often **clan-based or militant**, with hierarchies tied to blood potency or ancient deeds.
- They hold rites of passage like **Flame Trials**, **Sky Hunts**, or **Blood Challenges**.

They value:

- **Honor through strength and legacy**.
- **Dominance over destiny** — to kneel is to die.
- **Memory and inheritance**, carving their stories into stone, bone, or myth.

### 🏛️ Role in Global Politics

- They are core to the **Wyrmcoil Pact**, a powerful alliance of dragons, Wyrmblooded, and loyal beastkin.
- Feared by all factions but respected as elite warriors, especially by the **Choir of Ash** and **Hunters’ Accord**.
- Some oppose their own elders, fighting for freedom from the dragonlords’ iron traditions.

### **Subtypes**

- **Highborn Draconids**: Noble, pure-blooded, nearly full-dragon in power and form.
- **Emberborn**: Feral warriors who erupt into fire or scale-clad berserkers.
- **Veilclaws**: Stealthy wyrmbloods who blend shadow and flame, often spies or assassins.
