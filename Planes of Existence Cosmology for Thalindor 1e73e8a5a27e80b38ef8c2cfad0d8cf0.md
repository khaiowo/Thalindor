# Planes of Existence / Cosmology for Thalindor

Owner: Phntm Drknss

### **1. The Mortal Plane (Thalindor)**

- **The World of Thalindor**: The physical, mortal plane where the majority of life exists. It is a fragmented landmass created by the **Shattering**, a catastrophe that split the original world into its current form. Magic, nature, and divine influences all shape the geography, history, and civilizations here.

---

### **2. The Celestial Realms**

- **Celestium (The Heavens)**: The highest and most pure realm, home to the **Celestial Gods** and beings of light, order, and divine grace. It is a land of floating sanctuaries, palaces of marble and gold, and a bastion of hope. The **Celestium Pact** is the dominant order here, with Archon **Lirael Sunborne** serving as the leader.
    - **Notable Features**:
        - **The Throne of Divinity**: The palace where the gods of light gather.
        - **The Pillars of Radiance**: Massive, luminous spires that connect Celestium to other divine realms.
        - **The Silver Sea**: A vast, shimmering ocean that borders the Celestial Realms, where only the purest souls are allowed to cross.
- **The Seraphine Isles**: Floating islands in the sky, a sacred and serene place for **Seraphim**—beings of divine power and guardians of the natural order. These islands are where celestial magic is birthed, and divine laws are formed.
    - **Notable Features**:
        - **The Sunlit Citadel**: A radiant fortress where the Seraphim conduct their sacred rituals and worship.
        - **The Radiant Grove**: A mystical forest where light bends to protect and nourish the land.
- **The Divine Heavens (The Eighth Heaven)**: A rarefied realm that is said to be accessible only to the most devout mortals or gods who ascend beyond the Celestium. The **Eighth Heaven** is where ultimate truths, the original divine design, and cosmic balance are maintained.

---

### **3. The Abyssal Realms**

- **The Abyss**: A vast, dark, and chaotic realm ruled by the **Abyssal Conflux**, a confederation of demons and infernal entities. The Abyss is a land of endless darkness, fire, and brimstone, where the laws of time and space are warped. It is a realm of corruption, destruction, and temptation, constantly attempting to break through into the mortal world.
    - **Notable Features**:
        - **The Throne of Despair**: The seat of power of the **Dreadmarquis Kael’zareth**, the leader of the Abyssal Conflux.
        - **The River of Souls**: A cursed river that flows through the Abyss, carrying the tortured souls of those who have fallen to infernal corruption.
        - **The Infernal Pits**: A region of constant, chaotic warfare, where demons fight for power and souls are forever tormented.
- **The Chasm of Eternal Night**: A deep, black chasm that exists in the Abyss, where time itself is fractured. It is home to many lost gods and beings of incredible power. Ancient beings, cursed to remain in the Abyss, dwell here.

---

### **4. The Void**

- **The Void**: A realm that exists outside of the known reality, an emptiness that devours both time and space. It is a place where **reality becomes fractured** and the laws of nature no longer apply. The **Voidsteppes**, a region of Thalindor, is closely linked to this realm, where time itself can shift unpredictably.
    - **Notable Features**:
        - **The Shifting Wastes**: A chaotic, unstable region where time loops and twists. Those who enter often lose their memories or emerge as different people entirely.
        - **The Maw of Eternity**: A black hole-like structure that is a gateway to the Void, often used by dark sorcerers and entities to escape into its endless abyss.
        - **The Lost Realms**: Fragments of realities and time lost to the Void. Some scholars and sorcerers believe it is a place where the fabric of existence itself is unraveled.
- **The Timeless Spheres**: A metaphysical layer within the Void, where the concepts of past, present, and future exist simultaneously but outside of linear time. This area is often navigated by the **Seers** and those seeking knowledge of **destiny** or **prophecies**.

---

### **5. The Underworld (Realm of Death)**

- **The Underworld**: A dark and cold realm where the souls of the dead go after they leave the mortal plane. The **Abyssal Conflux** often seeks to corrupt these souls, while the **Celestial Realms** work to guide them toward peace. Mortals who enter the Underworld must face their own sins or trials.
    - **Notable Features**:
        - **The River Styx**: A river that separates the realms of life and death. Souls must pay the ferryman to cross.
        - **The Hall of Judgment**: A massive palace where gods and divine entities judge the souls of the dead.
        - **The Dark Pits of Limbo**: A cold, uninhabited region in the Underworld where lost or wandering souls roam endlessly.
- **The Obsidian Keep**: A fortress where the **God of Death**, **Threnozar**, resides. It is a place of eternal stillness and death. Few have ventured into this place and lived to tell the tale.

---

### **6. The Dreamscape (Realm of Dreams and Nightmares)**

- **The Dreamrealm**: A shifting, intangible plane where thoughts, dreams, and nightmares manifest. This is a space where **mind and will** shape the environment. While most mortals cannot perceive it, powerful magic-users, dreamwalkers, and even some gods can enter and manipulate the Dreamrealm.
    - **Notable Features**:
        - **The Veil of Echoes**: A region where lost memories and forgotten dreams echo in infinite repetition. It is said that **dreamweavers** and **nightmare lords** can travel here to create illusions or feed on the energy of dreams.
        - **The Kingdom of Slumber**: A serene but eerie realm, ruled by an enigmatic figure, possibly a deity, who controls the sleep of mortals.
        - **The Abyss of Nightmares**: A nightmarish section of the Dreamrealm where horrors manifest into physical beings. Those who venture too deep into this area risk being trapped in their nightmares forever.

---

### **7. The Ethereal Plane (Spirit Realm)**

- **The Ethereal Plane**: A realm that exists parallel to the mortal world, where **spirits** and **souls** wander after death. This plane is a ghostly reflection of the world, where physical forms blur and spirits can communicate with the living. It’s also home to **elemental spirits** and **ancestral spirits** who may guide or torment mortals.
    - **Notable Features**:
        - **The Spirit Sanctuary**: A peaceful haven where benevolent spirits rest, waiting for their turn to aid or guide the living.
        - **The Veil of Souls**: A liminal space where the spirits of the dead pass through before reaching their final resting place. It is said to be filled with both peaceful and vengeful spirits.
        - **The Abyssal Gateway**: A thin veil between the **Abyss** and the Ethereal Plane, where infernal spirits and demons sometimes cross over to haunt the living.

---

### **Cosmic Relationships and Dynamics**

- **Balance of Power**: The **Celestial Realms** and the **Abyss** are in a constant tug-of-war for influence over the mortal realm. Their battles manifest in wars, divine interventions, and even catastrophic events like the **Shattering**.
- **Divine Influence**: Gods from various planes often intervene directly in Thalindor' affairs, though they do so in different ways. Some, like the **Seraphim**, actively protect mortals, while others, like those from the **Abyssal Conflux**, try to sow chaos and destruction.
- **Cross-Plane Travel**: Certain powerful individuals or artifacts, like the **Split Soul Katana**, can bridge the gap between planes, either through ritual magic or inherent properties that allow them to tear through the fabric of reality.
