# 🌟 Rising Stars of Elyndros – Fully Expanded

Owner: Phntm Drknss

### **1. Aurum Circle**

**Selric Dovayne**

- **Backstory:** A former street urchin turned scholar of the arcane, Selric Dovayne was taken in by the Aurum Circle at a young age after demonstrating his innate talent for soul-alchemy. Born with a rare gift to transmute emotions into magical constructs, Selric quickly gained a reputation for breaking the limits of traditional alchemy. While most in the Circle see him as a mere ‘experiment,’ Selric’s passion for combining body, spirit, and metal has garnered the attention of both the arcane elite and radical philosophers.
- **Conflict:** His young idealism clashes with the more pragmatic and cold-hearted ideals of the Circle’s leaders, such as High Arcanix Valdus Marr. His ability to create living constructs from emotions is seen as an unpredictable risk by the older generation.
- **Current Goals:** Selric seeks to craft a new form of “living metal” that could replace existing methods of soul-binding, and he is secretly working on a project to bring back a lost alchemical god through emotion-based rituals. His ambition and naivety may be his downfall—or his salvation.

### **2. Verdant Oath**

**Nara Leafwake**

- **Backstory:** Nara Leafwake was born into the ancient Veldwen Tribe, whose roots are deeply intertwined with the primal forces of Elyndros. She is a direct descendant of the druids who first forged the Verdant Oath in ages past. At an early age, Nara was able to commune with ancient forest spirits, some of whom still guard the Worldroots—the heart of the Verdant Oath. She proved herself during a catastrophic incursion from the Abyssal Conflux, where she tamed the Wildwyrm—a beast believed to be the living incarnation of the jungle itself.
- **Conflict:** Some see Nara as the true heir to the Archdruidess title, while others in the tribe believe she is too young and untested to lead. Her increasing powers have drawn the attention of the more conservative factions within the Verdant Oath, who fear her growing connection to forces beyond their control.
- **Current Goals:** Nara aims to forge a new pact between the Verdant Oath and the untamed spirits of the jungle. However, as she strengthens this bond, whispers of an ancient and dangerous forest god’s revival have begun to spread, threatening the balance she is trying to protect.

### **3. Hunters’ Accord**

**Finn “Ghoststep” Marrow**

- **Backstory:** Finn was once an infamous assassin and treasure hunter known for his ghostly ability to vanish from sight, earning him the name “Ghoststep.” However, his methods became too ruthless even for the cutthroat Hunters’ Accord, and he left the mercenary faction, only to return years later as a changed man. Finn’s reputation has grown, but his loyalty to the Accord remains a constant tug-of-war.
- **Conflict:** His inner conflict between seeking redemption and remaining the cold, calculating hunter he once was creates tension within the Hunters’ Accord, especially with Drix Calshad, the faction leader, who views him as a loose cannon. Finn’s strong desire to undo past mistakes has led him to accept increasingly dangerous bounties, each one potentially pushing him closer to crossing a line from which he might not return.
- **Current Goals:** Finn has been pursuing a legendary bounty—a secret, powerful artifact thought to be able to rewrite fate itself. If he succeeds, it could change the course of history... but at what cost?

### **4. Hollowlight Archive**

**Vessira Quinth**

- **Backstory:** Vessira Quinth is a prodigy of chronomancy, the magical art of manipulating time. She was raised by the Archive to serve as an assistant to Archivist Enmara, but her talents quickly exceeded expectations. Through a series of dangerous experiments, she has learned to read the echoes of time left behind on objects, allowing her to perceive events that have happened long before her birth. This ability has led her to discover secrets thought to be lost to the world.
- **Conflict:** Vessira’s methods are controversial, and some fear that meddling with time’s fabric could have catastrophic consequences. Her rivalry with other mages—especially those who follow the rules of strict temporal magic—has led her down a dark path, one where even the Archive’s leadership is unsure of how far she should be allowed to go.
- **Current Goals:** Vessira is seeking the truth behind the disappearance of the Godscribe vaults, where forbidden knowledge and prophecies are stored. It is rumored that inside those vaults lie the answers to the greatest mysteries of Elyndros, but unlocking them may come at a terrible price.

### **5. Shardbound Covenant**

**Tazien Vohl**

- **Backstory:** Tazien Vohl is a genius engineer and sigil crafter with a penchant for combining magic and technology. A self-taught prodigy, he has pushed the boundaries of the Covenant’s sigil technology to unthinkable levels. His creations—artificial golems, unstable arcane weapons, and mana-harnessing devices—are both revered and feared by those who have seen them in action.
- **Conflict:** Tazien’s innovations have attracted the attention of darker forces within the Covenant, particularly the more authoritarian factions that wish to use his creations for war and domination. His increasing involvement with forbidden magic-tech experiments has put him at odds with those who wish to keep the Covenant’s magical practices ‘pure.’
- **Current Goals:** Tazien seeks to create the “Sigilcore”—a living construct that would be both magical and technological in nature, merging mind, metal, and magic into one entity. Some believe this could change the fate of Elyndros itself. Others think it might destroy the very fabric of reality.

### **6. Choir of Ash**

**Kaedra Ashveil**

- **Backstory:** Kaedra Ashveil was born in the wake of a cataclysmic event—the entire city of Brondar was destroyed in a firestorm. She was the sole survivor, a little girl who learned the art of flame-dancing as a way to protect herself and honor the ashes of her lost home. She has become a living symbol of the Choir of Ash’s fervent belief that fire cleanses and purifies the world.
- **Conflict:** Kaedra’s fire-based powers are uncontrollable at times, leading her to accidentally set entire forests ablaze. Her ability to commune with the spirits of the dead on the battlefield also leads to dangerous territory, as she begins to question whether she is truly guided by righteous flame or driven by an insatiable hunger for vengeance.
- **Current Goals:** Kaedra seeks to wield her fire in a controlled, sacred manner and become the true leader of the Choir of Ash. But her internal struggles with her powers and the past may cause her to embrace an even darker path—one where fire is not just a purifier but a harbinger of destruction.
