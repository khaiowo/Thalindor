# Legends & Prophecies in Thalindor

Owner: Phntm Drknss

1. Prophecy of the Broken Sky
One of the oldest and most mysterious prophecies in Thalindor, spoken in fragmented verses and spread across various ancient ruins and temples. It is said that a day will come when the sky itself fractures, and from the rift, an ancient terror—one thought to be long dead—will emerge. This event is believed to mark the return of Threnozar, the God of Stillness and Ends, or another entity of his ilk, bringing time to a halt.
Key Players: This prophecy involves the Aurum Circle, who have studied the fragments and fear that the rift could either reorder or destroy time itself.Their legendary hero is Mira Valeheart, The Platinum Saint—a paragon of soul-alchemy who forged harmony between body, spirit, and arcane metal.
Impact: Many believe Doki, having killed Threnozar, plays a pivotal role in either stopping or hastening this calamity.

---
2. The Seraphine Veil
An ancient prophecy whispered among the Seraphim and Celestium Pact. It foretells the arrival of a mortal with the soul of a Seraph, who will bridge the gap between the gods and mortals and unite the two realms. This figure will wield the Divine Lattice, a mysterious artifact thought to be a gift from the gods, and bring an age of peace—or endless war.
Key Players: The Celestium Pact, guided by the celestial ideals once upheld by Haldran the Shardwing—an angelic champion whose shattered wings reflect every judgment he’s ever cast.
Impact: The Divine Lattice, if real, could allow a mortal to rewrite divine law—or invite the wrath of heaven and hell alike.

---
3. The Curse of Zorathar
The Verdant Oath and Ashveil Ranges both hold pieces of this prophecy. It speaks of an ancient guardian—Zorathar the Sleeper—whose wrath will awaken if the balance of nature is utterly sundered. The “end of silence” will come when the land itself demands retribution.
Key Players: Korghul the Redhorn, a primal juggernaut who once wrestled a worldroot beast bare-handed to protect sacred groves, is still revered by the druids as the embodiment of raw, natural strength.
Impact: If Zorathar rises, a primordial reckoning would follow—nature’s final answer to unchecked ambition.

---
4. The Eternal Flame
This prophecy burns within the Choir of Ash, speaking of the Emberborn, a vessel of divine fire who will cleanse or consume the world. The chosen will awaken the First Flame and burn through gods and sins alike.
Key Players: The mythic Seren Embermaid, a flame-dancer said to burn away doubt and sin with a single waltz through battlefield flames, is believed to have once glimpsed the First Flame in a vision.
Impact: The Emberborn could bring an age of purifying fire or catastrophic destruction.

---
5. The Silent Song
A rare prophecy guarded by the Hollowlight Archive. It speaks of a song older than words, sung by one who bears no voice or past. It is said to contain truths that unravel gods and rewrite the origins of Thalindor.
Key Players: Tholun Dreadscribe, a lorebound prophet who sealed away a god’s whisper in living ink upon his own skin, gave his body to the Archive as a vessel of silence.
Impact: Should this song be heard, it could reveal forbidden truths and awaken sealed gods best left forgotten.

---
6. The Everfrost Cycle
From the icy hearts of the Frostmourne Tundra comes this grim legend: an eternal winter looms, heralded by the White Walker and halted only by the rise of a Frostborn—a mortal who commands the cycle of death and rebirth.
Key Players: Though not aligned with a formal faction, many believe the Frostborn echoes the legacy of Raya Quill, The Phoenix-Eyed—a huntress famed for capturing bounty after bounty with eyes that saw through deception and death.
Impact: This prophecy could freeze the land and awaken the dead, unless the Frostborn ends the cycle.

---
7. The Sigil's Last Echo
A prophecy held in hushed tones by the Shardbound Covenant. It foretells the day when a living prison of forbidden spells—Auri of the Living Sigil—shall either break or be broken. When that happens, reality itself may tear.
Key Players: Auri, a spellwright prodigy whose body became a prison of forbidden spells, never broken. Her existence is both a miracle and a curse, bound by sigils she carved into herself.
Impact: The resulting chaos could erase or reshape the arcane foundations of Thalindor.

---
8. The Word That Ends Worlds
A final, abyssal prophecy whispered by the Abyssal Conflux and feared by all. It speaks of a Voidtongue, one who can speak uncreation into being. The prophecy hints that her voice was once a song of beauty, now a dirge of annihilation.
Key Players: Siva Voidtongue, a cursed war-singer who speaks damnation into the hearts of gods and demons alike. Her whispers once sank cities and unraveled prayers mid-sentence.
Impact: If her voice ever escapes again, the fabric of existence itself may fray.

# Where Are They Now? The Living Legends of Thalindor

Despite the passing of eras, the legendary heroes of Thalindor did not fade into history—they endure, walk among the factions they once helped shape, or wander the world as myths made flesh. Whether through divine blessing, arcane preservation, or sheer defiance of time, they are still alive, their influence looming large.

1. Mira Valeheart (Aurum Circle)Now the elusive Grand Artifex Emerita, Mira resides in a soul-forged sanctum beyond time, occasionally appearing to teach lost techniques or intervene in crises that threaten the soul-weave. Her presence is rare—but unmistakable.

2. Korghul the Redhorn (Verdant Oath)A nomadic giant, Korghul roams the wildlands, guarding sacred sites and challenging would-be conquerors to primal duels. Druids claim he communes with Zorathar’s dreams—and wakes furious when nature bleeds.

3. Raya Quill (Hunters’ Accord)Still taking contracts, Raya has become a ghost story among bounty hunters. They say she only accepts jobs that tie into deeper threads of fate—and those she hunts often vanish without trace, their sins burned into ash behind her.

4. Tholun Dreadscribe (Hollowlight Archive)Bound to the Archive by the runes on his flesh, Tholun rarely speaks. He is now more scripture than man, a living reliquary consulted only when reality itself begins to fray.

5. Auri of the Living Sigil (Shardbound Covenant)Kept in a floating sanctum-prison overseen by the Shardbound, Auri still breathes—though she never speaks. Her skin glows with forbidden glyphs that pulse to ancient rhythms. She is both a guardian and a doomsday contingency.

6. Seren Embermaid (Choir of Ash)Rumored to have walked into an active volcano and returned alive, Seren leads an elite flamebound order. Her battlefield dances are now reserved for wars of moral weight, where fire is needed not just to destroy—but to purify.

7. Haldran the Shardwing (Celestium Pact)Still judging. Still feared. Haldran’s wings glisten like broken glass, and his mere presence silences lies. He acts only when divine balance is at stake—but when he does, entire wars end in a single descent.

8. Siva Voidtongue (Abyssal Conflux)Alive, but never welcome. Siva wanders the edge of creation, where reality bleeds into the void. Her voice remains sealed—but even her hums unravel courage. Some say she sings to the stars, daring them to die.
